### about

The mHuBERT-147 models are multilingual compact and efficient self-supervised speech representation models. They were trained and evaluated at NAVER LABS Europe with the funding of the UTTER EU project. They are shared under the license **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0**. For more information, check our publication [here](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.06371).
