<section class="project-category">
  <h2 class="category">About</h2>
  <p>
    This very small parallel speech corpus presents speech in the endangered language Griko and translations to Italian. 
    It is made of 330 sentences, with the following information levels: speech, machine extracted pseudo-phones, transcriptions, translations and sentence alignment.
  </p>
</section>
